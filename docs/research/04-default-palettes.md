## Method
- **Contrast**: computed with a small Bun script (WCAG 2.x relative luminance). Checks: brand vs `neutralLightBg`, and `brandForeground` vs brand. The script darkens the brand in OKLCH lightness (0.01 steps) until both pass; no palette needed adjusting after hand-tuning (e.g. GOV.UK blue nudged to `#1D6FB5`, Solarized yellow taken down to `#7A5F00`, Strava-style orange to `#B83B0A`).
- **neutralHue** is derived from the bg seeds in OKLCH (`hue`, max `chroma`), plus a written `intent`. The app should build the grey ramp at that hue/chroma rather than using stock zinc/slate.
- **Dark theme**: `contrast.brandOnDarkBg` is informational and mostly below 3:1. These brands are tuned for light surfaces, so the expander **must lighten brand for dark mode** (raise OKLCH L until ≥4.5:1 on the dark bg, then choose a dark foreground). Accent and status seeds are fills, not text colours. Derive text variants per theme and re-check them.
- **Diversity**: brand hues cover ~6°–264° OKLCH across red, raspberry, orange, saffron, mustard, olive, pine, jade, turquoise, petrol, blue, navy, plum and greige. Dark-led: Noir Champagne, Solarized Dusk, Nordic Frost, Hi-Vis. Muted: Morandi, Werner Moss, Nordic Frost. Saturated: Memphis, Crayon Box, Bauhaus, Signal Orange.
- **Tells avoided**: no indigo/violet brands, no gradients implied, no stock Tailwind indigo-500/slate values, no #F4F1EA + #D97757, no #0B0B0B/#111 blacks (darkest is `#0C1A20`, which is tinted).
- **Inspirations** are cited per palette. Hues are adapted, not copied, from trademarked brands (FT, Wise, Strava, Liberty). Open systems (GOV.UK, Solarized, Nord) are used close to source under their licences.

## Flags (2 of 2 allowed)
- **Hi-Vis**: deliberately echoes *near-black + single acid accent* (graphite `#2A2D33` + safety yellow `#FFD100`). It is justified for industrial/logistics brands and should not be a generic default.
- **Solarized Dusk**: partial echo of the *cream paper* cluster (`#FDF6E3` ≈ `#F4F1EA`). The brand is mustard, not terracotta. Default this palette to dark.
- Near-misses, not flagged: Plum Market (red-purple, hue ~340, not indigo), Salmon Paper (salmon, not cream; avoid pairing with broadsheet layout), Nordic Frost (neutral close to slate), Signal Orange (saturated orange on cool grey, not terracotta on cream).
